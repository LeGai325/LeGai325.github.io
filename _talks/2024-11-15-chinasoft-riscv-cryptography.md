**中文报告题目：** 基于 RISC-V 处理核的国密算法快速运算

At the RISC-V forum of CCF ChinaSoft 2024, I presented our work on accelerating Chinese cryptographic algorithms on a RISC-V processing core.

The presentation explored hardware–software co-design for SM2, SM3, and SM4, including custom instructions and optimization of frequently used operations. The goal was to improve cryptographic computation efficiency for resource-constrained and low-power IoT devices.
