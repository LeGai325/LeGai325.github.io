**中文报告题目：** 我的 IETF126 经历：两份草案、两场汇报，以及仍在继续的研究

At CNIRC 2026, I shared my experience of contributing to two individual Internet-Drafts and presenting them in the INTAREA and V6OPS working groups at IETF 126 in Vienna.

The talk followed the path from research findings to draft preparation, working-group discussion, and subsequent revision. I discussed how feedback on tunnel-node security requirements and IPv6 Extension Header measurements helped clarify the scope of our proposals and identify further experiments. I also shared practical lessons for first-time IETF participants, including preparing concise presentations, answering questions, and following up on mailing lists.
